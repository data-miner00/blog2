I've relocated a few weeks ago as I've changed my job. I like the unit so far. It's a common room that shares the bathroom with 2 other tenants. However, I noticed something perculiar inside the bathroom.

Well, sorry to break it but the bathroom is super clean and it's not anything supernatural or extrordinary. When I first used the bathroom the first day I moved in, I saw there is a mosquito hovering around the sink area. I didn't bother about it much and continue washing my face and took a shower normally.

For the subsequent days, I kept bumped into this mosquito whenever I open the tap. It will fly eccentrically as if it lives in the tap and I've disturbed it from a peaceful sleep. It's a bit annoying, but I didn't really want to kill it or anything. It's also trying to live its own life no? However, I thought maybe my housemates will take "necessary actions" or it will go away on its own once it got bored here.

To my dismay, none of that didn't happen. It's the third week I am here and so is the mosquito. I even started to respect it's existence and perseverance.

However, something unexpected happened yesterday. While I was washing my face with the tap on, the mosquito accidentally flew into the running water and I witnessed it fell on the sink and eventually drift into the sewage along with the flowing water. Aghast, I yelled "oh no!", but I couldn't undo what had happened. I chanted a bit for the loss and went back my room.

This morning, when I was using the bathroom, the eerie peacefulness was palpable. I didn't see the mosquito anymore and a sense of sadness tinged within me, which is unexpected.
